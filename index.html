Hello!

I'm Paola, and I'm working on my PhD in theoretical physics in the quantum field theory group at the Max Planck Institute for Physics, where I'm supervised by Prof. Dr. Johannes Henn and co-superivsed by Prof. Dr. Lorenzo Tancredi.

These days I'm mostly thinking about surfaceology and the soft-collinear properties of Yang-Mills amplitudes, but I am also more broadly interested in the mathematical structure of scattering amplitudes and their applications to the real world, from collider physics all the way to large-scale structure!

Education

BSc in Physics (Trento), MSc in Theoretical and Mathematical Physics (LMU & TUM Munich), and now a PhD at the Max Planck Institute for Physics, starting autumn 2026.
